    Driver’s briefing

    How to race

    Drag across the track to choose a line between lanes. Tap the arrows or use A and D to change lanes. Hold Boost or Space for extra power. Each heat gives you five seconds of boost.

    Catch a tow

    The car ahead carries air forward. In its blue wake, the air hits you more slowly and your engine can build speed. Move out before you reach its bumper. Avoid the cones.

    Read the wind

    Crosswinds push the wake sideways and expose the flank of your car. Headwinds increase airspeed. At the same angle, doubling airspeed makes four times the drag.

    Finish each heat for 5, 3, 2 or 1 point. Most points after three heats wins. The final heat breaks ties.
