# Pocket Cabinet games

- Make the game world fill the entire visible viewport in portrait, landscape, desktop, and installed PWA modes. Size the play surface to the dynamic viewport and resize it when the viewport changes.
- Overlay the HUD, touch controls, navigation, menus, tutorials, and results on the game visuals. Keep the action visible behind them. Avoid page headers, sidebars, instruction strips, or control panels outside the play surface.
- Keep touch targets clear of device safe areas. A player should be able to play a complete round without scrolling the page.
- Check the layout during actual gameplay at phone and desktop sizes. Verify the play surface reaches all four viewport edges and the HUD and controls remain usable.
